Who I Am

Hello! I am Nausad Aalam, a dedicated student of Chartered Accountancy registered with The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). I completed my schooling at Adarsha Secondary School, Biratnagar-7, Morang, graduating with an outstanding 3.50 GPA (Grade A) in the Secondary Education Examination (SEE).

During my college years, I actively explored technology and led our college team to win the First Prize (Winner) at the KIST Inter-College IT Competition (2024).

Following my passion for commerce and finance, I cleared the competitive ICAI CA Foundation exam and am currently preparing for CA Intermediate examinations. My goal is to combine world-class chartered accountancy expertise with cutting-edge tech capabilities.
