Who I Am

Hello! I am Nausad Aalam, a passionate learner and Chartered Accountancy student registered with The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). I was raised in Siraha, Nepal, where I completed my foundational schooling and secondary education.

My academic journey started with a fascination for commerce, financial structures, and business mathematics. After completing my SEE and earning my +2 in Management/Commerce, I took on the prestigious challenge of Chartered Accountancy. Having successfully cleared CA Foundation, I am actively preparing for CA Intermediate examinations.

Beyond accounting ledgers and legal provisions, I have a deep curiosity for modern web design, frontend coding, and video content creation. I believe the future belongs to professionals who can speak both the language of finance and the language of technology.
